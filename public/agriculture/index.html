What we farm

Plants and animals.

Crop production

Vegetables, grains and fodder, grown with water and soil records kept season by season.

Livestock

Cattle, small stock and poultry, with health, feed and movement tracked per animal.

Cold chain

Cold storage and refrigerated transport, so produce reaches buyers at the quality it left the farm.

Agro-processing

Cleaning, grading, packing and processing that turns harvests into shelf-ready products.

Livestock farmers: our sister company's FlockSphere 360 keeps flock records on your phone.
