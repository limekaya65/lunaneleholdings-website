What we do

Three jobs, done carefully.

Group treasury

Cash, funding and ring-fencing for every Lunanele company, so one company's risk never becomes another's.

Income investments

Stakes in established, profitable businesses, including controlling stakes, that pay regular dividends.

Growth stakes

Minority stakes in growing businesses, with support from our sister companies in infrastructure, agriculture and technology.

What we look for

Businesses worth owning for years.

If this sounds like your business, send us a few lines. No pitch deck is needed to start.

  • Based in South Africa and already trading with real customers
  • Run by an owner who wants to stay involved
  • Up-to-date books and tax affairs
  • A clear use for new capital
  • A fit with what our sister companies do

What we are, and what we are not

We invest only the Lunanele group's own money. We do not take deposits, manage money for anyone else, sell investment products or give financial advice. If you are looking to invest your savings, please speak to a registered financial adviser.
